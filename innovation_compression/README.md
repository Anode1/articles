# Chaos Makes Many, Compression Keeps Few

**Innovation admits a single description: chaos and recombination generate candidates
without limit, the few that survive are those that compress a structure many share, which is
also why they spread, and a population sifts them in parallel.**

The account splits the work into variation, now a cheap machine commodity, and the selecting
prior, the inductive bias that decides what to keep. Recombinant growth, the bias-variance
decomposition and cultural evolution are read as one process under that lens.

The grounding is a regress, formally motivated but not itself a theorem. A learner gains
nothing from data without a prior the data cannot supply (No-Free-Lunch), and the halting
problem, Godel's incompleteness and Rice's theorem, one limit in three guises, show that no
system certifies its own foundations from within. A system can change its inductive bias,
and meta-learning does; what it cannot do is warrant the change without appeal to a further
bias it also did not derive. At every level the commitment is imported rather than produced,
which is where the human part sits.
