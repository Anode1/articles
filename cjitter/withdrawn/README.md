# Withdrawn

Two papers on the same machinery, neither to be cited or extended.

`layout.tex` (Incremental Graph Layout by Stochastic Search) is withdrawn on its own first
page: after a repair defect was fixed, 1,118 of 1,200 per-seed values changed, the only
comparisons surviving Holm were against a heuristic the matched-budget control already
voids, and the instance carrying the refutation verdict moved. `reference.tex` is the
reframing that followed, abandoned because eight migrations of one schema are too narrow a
subject. `data/` and `figures/` belong to them. `PREREGISTRATION.md` governed `layout.tex`;
2A and 2B were successors never signed. `QUESTIONS.md` is the map of what the harness could
test, written before the pivot.

What survived: the library at github.com/Anode1/cjitter, and the question in
`../PREREGISTRATION-STATIONARITY.md`.
