# The Imposed and Emergent Pieces of Convolution Under an Energy Budget

**Which pieces of a convolution must be imposed as priors and which emerge on their own,
mapped by growing a network's topology from an exhaustive fully-connected seed under an
evolutionary budget that pays for every connection.**

The search is used as a lens rather than an optimizer. Sparse task-relevant connectivity
emerges cleanly. Weight sharing is adopted when translation invariance rewards it. The
compact aligned local filter emerges only once mutation acts on the shared feature the way
real genetics does, rather than on a single edge. Composition on the channel axis does not
emerge from the task label alone, and in two dimensions that axis meets a capacity wall at
three operations which four interventions fail to move. On top of the priors a ConvNet
hardwires, locality and translatability, the free dimensions organize to the task: compact
fields, and a depth that grows with the required receptive field while overshooting it.

A connection cost as a lens on emergent structure has precedent. What is new is the
decomposition, one mechanism it turns up (a compact filter appears only under whole-feature
mutation), and the negatives kept in.

`data/` holds the per-generation run data. The engine is
