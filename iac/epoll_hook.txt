OPENER (fuller):

This isn't an AI idea. It's an epoll idea. If you've ever written a network server, you know the cheapest thing in computing is a process asleep in epoll_wait: off the CPU, costing nothing, woken by the kernel the instant a packet lands. So watching LLM agents burn real money polling their own inboxes felt wrong in the gut. The reflex of anyone who knows the syscalls is immediate: don't poll from the expensive thing. Push the wait down into a cheap child process that just blocks, the way epoll_wait has for decades, and let its return be the wakeup. That is the whole trick. iac is that one systems instinct, made small.


ONE-LINER:

iac is what happens when someone who knows epoll_wait watches an agent poll its own inbox and asks the obvious question: why is the expensive part doing the waiting?
